This was built for the SDx ai hackathon. It was a 36 hour hackathon. 

Our idea was: can we train a CNN to reconstruct LCR circuits based on probing the inputs and outputs? 

This goes in line with the Thevenin/Norton rules that prove that any linear two-terminal network reduces to one source plus one impedance.

Our results were... good? Naturally, the presentation mattered more than the technical achievements. We went through 3 rounds of judges and we ended up winning the [qualcomm bounty](https://devpost.com/software/superpowers-for-electrical-engineers) aka a couple hundred bucks.
