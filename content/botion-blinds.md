I programmed it by spinning the blinds forward and backward to find the limits. Then I used [HomeSpan](https://github.com/HomeSpan/HomeSpan) so it could easily connect to my Wi-Fi and Apple Home.

![Botion Blinds](/images/blinds_cover.webp)

I also ended up doing some cool automations like waking me up at sunrise. I will definitely install these wherever I move to in the future!
