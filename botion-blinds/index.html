Botion Blinds

Goal: "hey siri, open the blinds"

I started with a very simple CAD & electronics setup that was simple and bulletproof.

BOM

QtyPartLink
1NEMA 17 stepperany
1PD Stepperthingsbyjosh
13DP colletCAD
13DP mountCAD
4M3 boltsany

I programmed it by spinning the blinds forward and backward to find the limits. Then I used HomeSpan so it could easily connect to my Wi-Fi and Apple Home.

I also ended up doing some cool automations like waking me up at sunrise. I will definitely install these wherever I move to in the future!
