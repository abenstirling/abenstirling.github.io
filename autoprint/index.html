Autoprint

This was a startup I did alongside 3 other friends my senior year in high school.

The mission was to simplify 3D printing.

Before launching on Kickstarter we did the following:

  1. Register a Delaware C-Corp
  2. File provisional patent
  3. Have a product demo & marketing materials ready

The coolest feature we built was a "one-click print", which somehow you still can't do today (due to slicing being done on computers, not the printers)

We ultimately raised $11k out of $30k, which is an unsuccessful Kickstarter campaign. We wound the company down shortly thereafter, but it was a ton of fun and a great effort from the team.
